# Clay, Fire, and Glaze

> A finished bowl carries evidence of wet hands, mineral color, and a kiln atmosphere that can never be repeated exactly.

### A closer look

1. Speckled stoneware reveals iron-rich grains after firing.
2. A thin glaze breaks lighter across rims and carved lines.
3. Small variations at the foot often say more about the maker than a perfectly smooth wall.

### In brief

Observations and small curiosities concerning handmade ceramics, subtle glaze surfaces, and the marks left by clay and heat. The charm of studio pottery lies in useful forms that still preserve the touch of their making.
